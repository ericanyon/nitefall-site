Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

The short version: Nitefall collects no data. Nothing leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Nitefall does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information.

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking SDKs.
  • No network code at all — the app never makes a network request.

What stays on your device

Everything the app remembers is local to your device — your nights, your streak, your theme unlocks, and your settings. It lives only in Nitefall's on-device data and is removed when you delete the app.

Health data

Nitefall does not currently read or write HealthKit data. If a future update ever reads sleep data, it will be read-only and will never leave your device — it will not be uploaded, synced, or shared.

Children

Nitefall collects no data from anyone, including children. The app is not targeted at children.

Third parties

None. Nitefall includes no advertising networks, no analytics providers, and no third-party trackers.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page. Because Nitefall collects no data, there is nothing to share, transfer, or sell — this policy can only get more private, not less.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@nitefall.app.
