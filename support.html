Support

Nitefall is a sleep-sounds app built for the dark. Choose a soundscape, tap "Tonight," and let it gently fade you out. No account, no ads, no tracking, and no subscription — everything stays on your device.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by Nitefall. Request one through your App Store account (App → Account → Purchase History → Report a Problem).

How does the fade work?

The fade is a sleep timer. Set how long you want the sound to play, and Nitefall eases the volume down to silence over a gentle final fade, then ends the session cleanly — no jarring stop.

Does Nitefall need the internet?

No. Nitefall makes no network requests at all. It works fully offline, in Airplane Mode, forever.

Is there a subscription?

No. No subscription, no ads, no account — just sleep.

Who made this?

A tiny family workshop. We built Nitefall to be the one thing we actually wanted at 2am: a warm, on-device nightlight for the ears.
